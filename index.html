Zuul ticket reader

This chat is readable by the Zuul SCM CI team's PO toolkit. It reads messages so that service incidents and requests reported here can be drafted into Jira tickets, with a link back to the conversation. A person reviews every ticket before it is created.

  • It can read this chat's messages only, not any other chat.
  • It cannot post, edit or delete anything in Teams.
  • Message content may be processed by an AI assistant (Cursor) while drafting.

To revoke access, remove the app from this chat. Questions: Mihai Borsu (mihai.borsu@nokia.com).
